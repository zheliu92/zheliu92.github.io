Zhe Liu (刘哲) is a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at the [University of British Columbia](https://www.ubc.ca/), advised by Prof. [Joanna McGrenere](https://www.cs.ubc.ca/~joanna/). Her research focuses on designing and evaluating **human–AI collaboration** systems that enhance human capabilities. She is especially interested in using AI to support **complex professional and creative tasks**, drawing on methods from **human–computer interaction (HCI)** and **natural language processing (NLP)**. Her current work explores real-time AI assistance for qualitative research methods, particularly semi-structured interviews. Previously, she studied **multi-device collaborative interaction** and technology design for **older adults**.
