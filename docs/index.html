About Me

Zhe Liu (刘哲) is a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at the University of British Columbia, advised by Prof. Joanna McGrenere. Her research focuses on designing and evaluating human–AI collaboration systems that enhance human capabilities. She is especially interested in using AI to support complex professional and creative tasks, drawing on methods from human–computer interaction (HCI) and natural language processing (NLP). Her current work explores real-time AI assistance for qualitative research methods, particularly semi-structured interviews. Previously, she studied multi-device collaborative interaction and technology design for older adults.
